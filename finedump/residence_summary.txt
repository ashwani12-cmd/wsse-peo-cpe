READING THESE NUMBERS
  'occupancy' is the mean number of Li+ in contact per frame and
  must match Table 1 of the paper at the same cutoff -- it does,
  which confirms the two analyses are consistent.
  Decomposing occupancy = visit rate x mean residence separates a
  thermodynamic preference (longer stays, deeper well) from a
  kinetic/geometric one (more arrivals). With 50 ps resolution the
  split is indicative; confirm it with a short fine-dump rerun.
