IN-PLANE TRANSPORT FROM THE EXISTING 30 ns TRAJECTORY
========================================================================
  fit window              : 3.0-10.0 ns, MSD_xy = 4 D t (2D)
  D_Li+   (in-plane)      : 4.401e-07 +/- 1.2e-09 cm^2/s
  D_TFSI- (in-plane)      : 1.167e-06 +/- 2.6e-09 cm^2/s
  D_TFSI / D_Li           : 2.65
  diffusion exponent beta : Li+ 0.68, TFSI- 0.92   (1.0 = fully diffusive)

  ideal Nernst-Einstein t+ : 0.274 +/- 0.001
     (neglects ion correlation; an upper bound for PEO/LiTFSI)

  electrolyte volume      : 76800 A^3  (58% of the cell; the rest is vacuum + WSSe)
  sigma (Nernst-Einstein) : 6.029e-03 S/cm
     (ideal NE, in-plane D only; an upper bound)

  INTERPRETATION
  * D_TFSI/D_Li > 1 is the expected ordering for PEO/LiTFSI: the
    cation is retarded by ether-oxygen coordination while the
    delocalised anion moves more freely. Recovering it supports
    the 0.75 charge-scaling choice.
  * Both species are sub-diffusive (beta < 1), so these are
    apparent diffusivities over the 3-10 ns window, as for Li+.
  * t+ and sigma are ideal-NE values and should be quoted as
    upper bounds, not as converged predictions.
