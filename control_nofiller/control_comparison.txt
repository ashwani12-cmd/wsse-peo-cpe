HOW TO READ THIS
  * D_xy(Li+): a difference here is the filler's effect on in-plane
    mobility. The composite is expected to be SLOWER if the filler
    obstructs, or similar if it only blocks the cross-plane channel.
  * n_coord and the Li-O peak should be UNCHANGED. If they are, the
    claim that the filler is a structural rather than chemical
    perturbation is established rather than asserted.
  * P2 near the sheet vs the control's bulk value is the clean test
    of templating: in the control there is no sheet, so its 'near'
    and 'bulk' values should agree with each other.
  * D_TFSI/D_Li in the control is the force-field validation against
    neat PEO/LiTFSI literature, free of any filler contribution.
